# Rev B front board

The 72 × 100 mm carrier holds the QR engine, status lighting, NFC, controller and relay. Open [the KiCad project](pcb/Pod-B.kicad_pro) for the electrical design and [the SolidCraft placement model](pod-v2-front-board.sc) for the front and rear. Rebuild the model with `python3 ../tools/build_front_board.py` from this directory.

The [SolidCraft exploded assembly](pod-v2-rev-b-exploded.sc) imports the [populated KiCad board](pcb/Pod-B-populated.step). The scanner and NFC module show their outer dimensions. The enclosure and light guide are concepts pending a physical fit check. Rebuild the assembly with `python3 ../tools/build_rev_b_exploded.py`.

- **Front.** The Newland EM3080-W V2 scanner is 15.5 mm wide, 9.7 mm high and 13.9 mm deep. A cradle holds it behind the cutout. Its 12-pin flex terminates on the carrier. Verify fastener clearance and cable bend with a sample. Twelve addressable RGB LEDs surround the window. A light guide blends their output. Place an opaque divider between the ring and scanner. [Newland scanner](https://www.newland-id.com/en/products/scan-engines/em3080-w-v2) · [Integration drawings](https://newlandemea.katanapim.com/AttachmentDownload/Download/688?name=EM3080-W+V2_Integration+Guide_V1+2026.pdf)
- **NFC.** The carrier holds an ELECHOUSE PN7160 module with its own antenna under the tap area. The coil shown in SolidCraft indicates placement. Verify tag and phone read range in the final enclosure with the LED ring on. Use authenticated DESFire EV3 credentials for fobs. [ELECHOUSE module](https://www.elechouse.com/docs/pn7160/) · [DESFire EV3](https://www.nxp.com/products/MF3DHx3)
- **Rear.** ESP32-C3 with edge-overhanging Wi-Fi antenna, optional dry-contact relay, power conversion and the scanner flex connector. Size the 3.3 V rail for simultaneous Wi-Fi and scanner peaks. Size the LED supply for the full ring and verify NFC range at maximum ring brightness.

For the first phone test, the app reads a short-lived NFC challenge from the pod. The online backend verifies the signed-in member, the pod and the unused challenge before authorizing entry. Android can also emulate a contactless credential. iPhone credential emulation requires an eligible use case and Apple entitlement. [Android HCE](https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/nfc/hce) · [Apple HCE in the EEA](https://developer.apple.com/support/hce-transactions-in-apps/)

Before connecting a door, check rails under simultaneous scanning, Wi-Fi transmission and full ring brightness. Verify the scanner flex pin order with a continuity test. Check that the relay stays open at boot, reset and power loss, using a dummy input. Physical fit, optical and electrical validation, and credential firmware are pending.
