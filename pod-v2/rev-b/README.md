# Rev B front board

This SolidCraft placement study puts the QR engine, status lighting, NFC, controller and relay on one carrier. The 72 × 100 mm outline is an estimate. Rebuild with `python3 ../tools/build_front_board.py` from this directory. The saved views show the front and rear.

- **Front.** The Honeywell N4680 scanner envelope is 21.15 × 11.5 × 14.6 mm. Mount the module to the carrier and expose the lens through a cutout. Its flex connector terminates on the same PCB. The lens position, fasteners and flex bend remain estimates until the integration drawing or a sample is available. Twelve addressable RGB LEDs surround the window. A light guide blends their output and an opaque divider should block ring light from reaching the scanner. [Honeywell N4680](https://automation.honeywell.com/au/en/products/productivity-solutions/barcode-scan-engines-and-modules/2d-barcode-scan-engines/n4680-series-2d-decoded-scan-engine)
- **NFC.** Reserve a separate front-facing antenna area under the scanner. The illustrated winding is a placement reference. The PN7160 on the same board drives the tuned coil. Keep large copper pours and nearby metal out of the antenna region and tune it in the final enclosure. Use authenticated DESFire EV3 credentials for fobs. [PN7160](https://www.nxp.com/products/PN7160) · [Antenna guide](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN13219.pdf) · [DESFire EV3](https://www.nxp.com/products/MF3DHx3)
- **Rear.** ESP32-C3 with edge-overhanging Wi-Fi antenna, optional dry-contact relay, power conversion and the scanner flex connector. Size the 3.3 V rail for simultaneous Wi-Fi and scanner peaks. Size the LED supply for the full ring and verify NFC range at maximum ring brightness.

For the first phone test, the app reads a short-lived NFC challenge from the pod. The online backend verifies the signed-in member, the pod and the unused challenge before authorizing entry. Android can also emulate a contactless credential. iPhone credential emulation requires an eligible use case and Apple entitlement. [Android HCE](https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/nfc/hce) · [Apple HCE in the EEA](https://developer.apple.com/support/hce-transactions-in-apps/)

This folder contains the mechanical placement study. Electrical routing, antenna tuning, optical and electrical validation, and credential firmware are pending.
